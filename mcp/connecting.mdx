---
title: "Connect an assistant"
description: "Add XCS to your AI assistant."
icon: "plug"
---

1. Open your assistant's settings and add an MCP connection.
2. Enter this server URL:

   ```text
   https://api.restrafes.co/api/xcs/mcp
   ```

3. Sign in to your XCS account.
4. Review the requested access and approve the connection.

If your assistant asks for an OAuth client ID, request one from XCS support.

Once connected, try asking:

> List my organizations and show the access points in the Lobby location.

Your assistant discovers the [available tools](/mcp/tools) automatically. To use remote unlock, first complete [Remote Unlock Setup](/guides/remote-unlock-setup).
