Edit the Luau table returned by the supplied `CONFIGURATION` ModuleScript, then restart Play. Hera fills omitted fields from its defaults and normalizes supported legacy keys. Merge snippets into the existing section and preserve any other overrides. [Defaults and aliases](/hera/reference/configuration)

## Choose a section

| Section | What it changes | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `NAME`, `MAIN` | Elevator identity and primary floor | [Settings reference](/hera/reference/configuration#identity-and-main-floor) |
| `MOVE` | Travel, leveling, pre-opening, counterweight | [Movement](/hera/configuration/motion) |
| `DOORS` | Door profiles, dwell, sensors and nudging | [Doors](/hera/configuration/doors) |
| `BUTTONS` | Button lights, colors, and materials | [Buttons](/hera/configuration/buttons) |
| `SOUNDS`, `VOICE` | Effects, chimes, and announcements | [Sound and voice](/hera/configuration/sound-and-voice) |
| `API` | Floor labels and timing for displays and chimes | [Indicators and labels](/hera/configuration/indicators) |
| `QUEUE` | Delay before dispatch and direction after calls | [Calls and dispatch](/hera/operation/dispatch-and-calls) |
| `FIRE_SERVICE` | Recall floors and fire-service entrance | [Fire service](/hera/operation/fire-service) |
| `INDEPENDENT_SERVICE` | Independent-service availability and entrance | [Independent service](/hera/operation/independent-service) |
| `MISCELLANEOUS` | Door aliases, parking, passenger welding | [Buttons](/hera/configuration/buttons), [parking and passengers](/hera/operation/parking-and-welding) |
| `XCS` | Optional dashboard connection | [Connect XCS](/hera/integration/xcs) |

Use an [extension](/hera/extensions/getting-started) for runtime behavior such as maintenance lockouts or reacting to door state.
