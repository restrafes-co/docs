Use these instance names when building or copying an assembly. Leading dots are literal. Hera indexes the landings and entrances during startup, so restart Play after changes to the hierarchy.

## A two-floor elevator

The following tree shows one front entrance at two floors. Repeat the landing door structure at each served floor. The loader is included with your supplied model; its name may differ.

```text

├── CONFIGURATION (ModuleScript)
├── extensions (optional Folder)
│   └── Maintenance (ModuleScript)
├── CAR (Model)
│   ├── .PLATFORM (BasePart)
│   ├── .DOORS (Model)
│   │   └── 1 (Model: front door group)
│   │       ├── LEVEL (BasePart)
│   │       └── DOORS (Model)
│   │           ├── DL1 (Model)
│   │           │   └── SCALE (BasePart)

│   ├── .DOORSENSORS (Model)
│   │   └── 1 (Model)
```

## Car and floor reference parts

The stopping datums are the center Y of `CAR/.PLATFORM` and the center Y of `FLOORS/<floor>/LEVEL_MAIN`. The door group's `LEVEL` is its weld datum. Preserve the offset between the stopping datum, the weld datum and the sill when copying a landing.

Name floors with canonical integers such as `-1`, `0` or `3`. Numbers and `LEVEL_MAIN` heights must both increase from bottom to top. Skipped numbers are fine. Use [floor labels](/hera/configuration/indicators) for names like `L` or `P1`.

## Door groups

An entrance is available when its numeric group is present under both the car and the landing. Groups `0` and `-1` are reserved. With car groups `1` and `2`, a landing containing only group `2` is rear-only if your car uses the default front and rear layout. [Fit floors and entrances](/hera/adding-floors)

## Door leaves and travel distance

Place each leaf model directly under the group's `DOORS` model. Put its `SCALE` part and the parts that travel with it inside the leaf model.

| Prefix | Opening direction, relative to the door assembly | Dimension used for travel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DL` | Left | `SCALE.Size.X` |
| `DR` | Right | `SCALE.Size.X` |
| `DU` | Up | `SCALE.Size.Y` |
| `DD` | Down | `SCALE.Size.Y` |

The integer suffix is the travel multiplier. `DL1` travels one scaler width left and `DR2` travels two scaler widths right. Use whole-number suffixes.

For a center-opening pair, start with `DL1` and `DR1`. Keep the scaler's orientation aligned with the assembly. If a panel travels too far, check its scaler size and name before changing door speed.

## Sensors

Put touch-enabled, non-colliding sensor parts across the entrance in `CAR/.DOORSENSORS/<group>`. Match the numeric group to the door. In Play, obstruct one entrance with an avatar and observe that group reopen.

## Buttons and lights

Use `.BUTTONS` panels directly under `CAR` and the landings. Give each fixture a direct `BUTTON` part. Optional direct parts with names beginning with `LED` carry the lamp styles. Hera uses `BUTTON` as the lamp when no LED parts are present. [Fixture names and examples](/hera/configuration/buttons)

## Optional counterweight

Place `COUNTERWEIGHT` beside `CAR` and give it a `.MAIN` reference. Enable its movement in [counterweight settings](/hera/configuration/motion#counterweight-and-sheaves).

## Objects Hera creates

Hera creates `API`, `LEGACY`, welds and motor objects at runtime. Install your own ModuleScripts in the permanent lowercase `extensions` folder under the elevator root. [Write an extension](/hera/extensions/getting-started)
