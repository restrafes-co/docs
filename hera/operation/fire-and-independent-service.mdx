---
title: "Service modes"
description: "Hera's availability, dispatch and door behavior for fire and independent service."
icon: "siren"
---

| Mode | Dispatch | Doors at arrival |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Phase I](/hera/operation/fire-service#phase-i-recall) | Recall to the configured primary or alternate landing. | The recall entrance opens and holds. |
| [Phase II On](/hera/operation/fire-service#phase-ii-firefighter-control) | One COP destination at a time. | Constant-pressure Door Open. |
| [Phase II Hold](/hera/operation/fire-service#phase-ii-firefighter-control) | The car remains at the current landing. | The selected entrance holds open. |
| [Independent](/hera/operation/independent-service) | One COP destination at a time. Holding the destination can close the doors. | The selected entrance opens and holds. |

Fire and independent service are mutually exclusive. The configuration `ENABLED` fields make the mode available. Send the corresponding [service request](/hera/reference/service-api) to activate it from a server-side key switch or panel.
