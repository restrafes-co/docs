## Configure recall

Under `FIRE_SERVICE`, set `ENABLED = true`, `RECALL_FLOOR`, `ALTERNATE_RECALL_FLOOR` and the preferred `DOOR_GROUP`. Use numeric floor IDs from `FLOORS`. Omit `RECALL_FLOOR` to use `MAIN.PRIMARY_FLOOR`. Hera falls back to an available group if the preferred one is absent at recall.

## Connect the controls

Send mode and constant-pressure inputs from the server. The Phase I control selects alternate recall with `recallFloorUnavailable = true`. [Request fields](/hera/reference/service-api#fire-service-requests)

## Phase I recall

1. Turn off independent service, then enable Phase I and select the normal or alternate recall floor.
2. Verify cancellation of ordinary calls. A moving car can brake at an intermediate landing with its doors closed before running to recall.
3. At recall, verify the selected group opens and holds.

Extension floor locks yield to fire service. A stopped or faulted car can still be unable to recall. Read `onFireServiceCommandRejected` for the command and reason.

## Phase II firefighter control

| Position | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `on` | After completed recall, permits one car destination and held door controls. |
| `hold` | Holds the stationary car at the landing with the selected entrance open. |
| `off` | Returns to Phase I and recalls the car when needed. |

### Make a trip

After completed recall, select On and register one COP destination. Hold Door Close through closure. At arrival, hold Door Open through the opening stroke. Hera rejects hall and API pickups during Phase II.

### Release a control early

Releasing Door Close during closing reopens the entrance. Releasing Door Open during opening closes it again. A custom panel must deliver the release as well as the press. [Held controls](/hera/reference/service-api#held-controls)

### Hold at a landing

Select Hold while stationary. Select On to resume Phase II or Off to return to Phase I.

## Return to normal service

Set Phase II to Off. Wait for the car to finish recall at the selected landing, then disable Phase I. Confirm normal operation with a fresh hall call and car call.

## Check the installation

Test each recall landing and both early-release cases. Include one recall during motion and one with the car idle and doors open. [Installation tests](/hera/testing)
