Hera accepts calls from car buttons, hall buttons, and the controller API. Each call includes a floor and door group. Hall calls also include the requested direction.

## How calls are served

Hera uses collective operation:

1. The car continues serving calls in its current direction.
2. Calls ahead of the car are served when the car can stop for them.
3. Calls behind the car remain registered.
4. When no calls remain in the current direction, the car reverses to serve the remaining calls.

This is similar to the way a conventional passenger elevator answers several calls without reversing for every new request.

## Car and hall calls

- A car call selects a destination from inside the car.
- An up or down hall call requests travel in that direction.
- Pressing a button for the current floor opens or reopens the selected door group.
- Pressing an already registered button does not add a duplicate call or cancel the existing call.

Front and rear openings may have separate calls at the same floor.

## Accessible calls

An API call may set `accessibility = true`. Hera then uses the configured accessibility door and nudge timers for that stop.

## Call settings

`QUEUE.PROCESS_DELAY` adds a short delay before Hera begins processing a call. `QUEUE.RESET_DIRECTION_ON_FINAL_CAR_CALL` controls whether the direction indicator returns to neutral after the final car call.

Use the [controller API](/hera/integration/controller-api) to add, remove, or monitor calls from another server script.
