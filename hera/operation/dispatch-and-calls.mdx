A **call** asks the elevator to serve a floor and entrance. A car button chooses a destination from inside; a hall button asks for a car travelling up or down from a landing. Server scripts can add calls through the [controller API](/hera/integration/controller-api).

## Which call is served next

During normal operation, Hera serves calls along its current direction, then reverses when that sweep is complete.

For example, a car travelling up from floor 1 toward floor 5 can serve an Up call at floor 3 if it still has enough distance to stop. A Down call can remain registered until the car is ready to serve downward travel. A new call behind the car does not make it reverse immediately.

| Situation | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A compatible call is ahead and the car can stop in time. | Hera can serve it during the current trip. |
| A call is behind the moving car. | It stays registered for a later sweep. |
| A call arrives too late to stop at its floor. | The car continues and serves it later. |
| No calls remain ahead, but calls remain behind. | Hera reverses its serving direction. |
| The car reaches a terminal floor. | Its next serving direction points back into the served floors. |

**Travel direction** describes actual motion. **Queue direction** describes which direction the car is serving next. A lantern showing the next direction can therefore differ from an arrow showing current travel.

## Repeated and current-floor calls

Pressing an already registered button keeps its call. It does not cancel the call or create a second identical entry.

In normal service, a car call for the current floor can open or reopen the selected entrance. A hall call at the current floor also depends on the serving direction. A Down call is not necessarily answered immediately by a car committed to serving Up.

A call lamp can briefly flash for press feedback without remaining registered. Look for a lamp that stays lit and the resulting elevator behavior when checking a new fixture.

## Front and rear calls

Calls include a numeric door group. Floor 3 front and floor 3 rear are different entrances and can be requested separately.

When calls for both entrances match the stop Hera is serving, the car can open both available groups. A group missing from that landing cannot be used. See [Floors and door openings](/hera/adding-floors#choose-which-entrances-a-floor-serves).

## Calls needing more boarding time

A server-script call with `accessibility = true` uses the extended open and nudge times configured under `DOORS` for that stop. This does not create a special physical button automatically.

Set `ACCESSIBILITY_TIMER_LENGTH` and `ACCESSIBILITY_NUDGE_TIMER_LENGTH` in [Door settings](/hera/configuration/doors#time-with-the-doors-open), then test both a normal and an accessible call.

## Dispatch settings

| Setting | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `QUEUE.PROCESS_DELAY` | `1` | Seconds before dispatch processes a call. The car must still be ready and its doors closed. |
| `QUEUE.RESET_DIRECTION_ON_FINAL_CAR_CALL` | `false` | Returns the serving direction to neutral after the last car call when enabled. |

A registered call is not a guarantee of immediate movement. Open doors, a stop state, or an out-of-service condition can prevent departure.

## Calls during special service

| Service | Calls accepted |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | Car, hall, and permitted API calls. |
| Phase I recall | The recall trip; ordinary calls are rejected. |
| Phase II On | One car destination at a time. |
| Phase II Hold | No new travel destination. |
| Independent service | One car destination at a time under the operator's control. |

Fire and independent service cancel the normal calls they replace. Rejected service calls produce an event your script can use for feedback; see [Service-control API](/hera/reference/service-api#rejected-requests).

For ordinary cancellation from scripts, see [Remove a call](/hera/integration/controller-api#remove-a-call). Removing an active destination does not make the moving car reverse or stop between floors.
