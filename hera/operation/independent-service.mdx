---
title: "Independent service"
description: "Configure independent service and commission the constant-pressure COP sequence."
icon: "user-round-cog"
---

Set `INDEPENDENT_SERVICE.ENABLED = true` and choose the preferred `DOOR_GROUP`. With the car stationary and fire service off, enable independent service from the key switch or a [server-side panel](/hera/reference/service-api#independent-service-requests). Confirm that the queue clears, the entrance opens and `states.service` becomes `"independent"`.

## Depart with a held destination

1. Hold the COP destination until every entrance is fully closed, then release.
2. At arrival, verify the destination's selected entrance opens and holds.
3. Repeat and release the destination during closing. Hera cancels the departure and reopens the available entrances. An explicit Door Open also cancels a pending held-floor departure.

On a front-and-rear car, verify both groups close before departure. A custom COP must send paired press and release messages with `independentServiceFloorControl`, using the same floor and group.

## Use a separate Door Close control

Hold Door Close through closure, then register the destination. Use this sequence if the controller lacks held-floor support or the integration sends a plain `queueAdd` with source `"car"`.

## Turn independent service off

Clear independent service while stationary and register fresh calls. Hall and API pickups are rejected while independent service is active.

## If a trip does not start

Check the held floor and group, each door state, and any [floor lockout](/hera/extensions/floor-lockouts). A plain `queueAdd` using source `"car"` while the doors are open can return `floor_button_not_held`. [Rejection reasons](/hera/reference/service-api#rejected-requests)
