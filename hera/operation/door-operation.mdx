Each door group controls one entrance. Its car and landing panels operate together, while a second entrance can have a different state.

For model setup and timing values, use [Model structure](/hera/model-structure) and [Door settings](/hera/configuration/doors). This page explains what the controls do during use.

## A normal stop

1. The car slows and levels at its destination.
2. Chimes and direction lights operate at their configured times.
3. The requested car and landing doors open.
4. The open timer allows time to board or leave.
5. The doors close, then Hera can depart for the next call.

With pre-opening enabled, the doors may start opening just before leveling finishes. At a floor without a particular landing entrance, that door group stays closed.

## Door controls in normal service

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Door Open | Opens the selected entrance at the current landing, or reverses a normal closing cycle. |
| Door Close | Ends the normal wait and releases Door Hold so closing can begin. |
| Door Hold | Keeps the selected entrance open until Door Close releases the hold. |
| Current-floor car button | Can open or reopen that button's entrance. |

Fixture names such as `FDO`, `FDC`, and `FDH` choose the entrance through the configured alias. See [Buttons](/hera/configuration/buttons).

A door control cannot open a landing that does not exist at the car's floor. Normal controls are also restricted while the car is moving and during special service.

## Sensor reopening

During a normal close, a player character entering the sensor area makes the doors reopen. After reopening, the normal wait uses `REOPEN_TIMER_LENGTH`, or `TIMER_LENGTH` if no separate reopen time is set.

While a sensor remains obstructed, the normal countdown pauses or resets according to `TIMER_HOLD_MODE`. The nudge countdown continues, so repeated obstruction does not keep postponing nudging indefinitely.

Test using your avatar. A loose part is not treated as a passenger by Hera's standard sensor detection.

## Nudging

If the entrance remains occupied long enough, Hera starts its nudge warning and closes using the nudge speeds. Sensors no longer cause the doors to reverse during this close.

A nudge close is distinct from an ordinary close: the warning, speed values, and timeout are configured separately. Test the normal reopen behavior before testing the prolonged-obstruction case.

## Special-service door controls

| Service | Door behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Phase I fire recall | Doors close for recall and are held open at the recall landing. Normal passenger controls do not interrupt recall. |
| Phase II On | Hold Door Open or Door Close until the movement finishes. Releasing early reverses the movement. |
| Phase II Hold | Holds the stationary car at its landing with the selected doors open. |
| Independent service | Holds doors open at arrival. In 26.9.6, hold a destination floor button through closure to depart. |

Use the complete sequences in [Fire service](/hera/operation/fire-service) and [Independent service](/hera/operation/independent-service). The normal sensor-reopening sequence described above applies to normal service; do not assume it replaces the held-control rules in those modes.

## If a door stops partway

Check Output for a door failure before changing the timer. In 26.9.6, a failed door cycle can set the group to `fault` and put the elevator out of service. Fix the reported model or movement problem and restart. See [Troubleshooting](/hera/reference/troubleshooting#doors-stop-partway-or-the-elevator-goes-out-of-service).
