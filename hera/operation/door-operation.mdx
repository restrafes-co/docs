Hera tracks each entrance independently. A normal stop opens the selected groups. With pre-opening enabled, the selected entrance can begin opening during final leveling.

## Door controls in normal service

| Control | Hera behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Door Open | Opens the selected entrance at a served landing or requests a reversal during a normal close. |
| Door Hold | Latches the selected group open. |
| Door Close | Releases the hold and zeroes dwell. Normal closure waits for the sensor to clear. |
| Current-floor car button | Can open or reopen its entrance with the car stopped and level. |

For scripted controls, use [extension door methods](/hera/extensions/controller#doors). Watch `onDoorStateChanged` for the requested group to reach `open` or `closed`.

## Sensor reopening

The group sensor detects player characters during a normal close. After a reversal, dwell uses `REOPEN_TIMER_LENGTH` or falls back to `TIMER_LENGTH`. While held by the sensor, dwell pauses in `TIMER_HOLD_MODE = 1` and resets in mode `2`. See [obstruction sensor settings](/hera/configuration/doors#obstruction-sensors).

## Nudging

Obstruction leaves the nudge timer running. Door Hold and special service suspend the normal timers. At the nudge threshold, the warning sounds if enabled and the nudge close follows after `NUDGE_CLOSE_DELAY`. Normal sensor reversal is suppressed during that close. [Door settings](/hera/configuration/doors)

## Special-service door controls

Use the held controls documented for [fire service](/hera/operation/fire-service) and [independent service](/hera/operation/independent-service).

## If a door stops partway

A failed cycle can put the group in `fault` and take the car out of service. Record the floor and group from `onDoorFault` and use the first server Output error to locate the failure. [Door troubleshooting](/hera/reference/troubleshooting#doors-stop-partway-or-the-elevator-goes-out-of-service)
