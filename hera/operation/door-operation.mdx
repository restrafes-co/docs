Each door group operates independently. A front and rear opening can therefore have different states and serve different floors.

## Normal sequence

1. The car arrives and levels at the floor.
2. The arrival chime and direction indicators operate at their configured times.
3. The matching car and landing doors open.
4. The doors remain open for the configured dwell time.
5. The doors close and Hera continues to the next call.

Pre-opening may begin the opening sequence shortly before leveling is complete.

## Door controls

- Door open reverses a normal closing door or opens a closed door at the current floor.
- Door close ends the normal dwell time and releases door hold.
- Door hold keeps that door group open until door close is pressed.

Door controls apply only during normal operation.

## Obstructions

When a sensor detects a player during a normal close, the doors reopen. The reopened doors use `REOPEN_TIMER_LENGTH` before trying to close again.

The nudge timer continues while the entrance is obstructed. When it expires, Hera sounds the nudge warning and closes at the configured nudge speed. Sensors do not reverse a nudge close.

<Callout type="warning">
  Test each sensor and door group after changing the entrance geometry, timers, or closing speed.
</Callout>

See [Doors](/hera/configuration/doors) for the related settings.
