Use this guide after installation, after changing floors or doors, and before opening the experience to players. Start a new playtest after editing the model or `CONFIGURATION`.

Keep Output visible. If a test fails, record the floor, door group, direction, and first error message, then follow [Troubleshooting](/hera/reference/troubleshooting).

## Normal travel

| Test | What should happen |
| --- | --- |
| Call from every landing. | The matching hall button registers, the car arrives, and the correct entrance opens. |
| Select every destination from inside. | The car stops at the matching `LEVEL_MAIN` height and clears the call lamp. |
| Travel up and down. | Starting, slowing, and leveling work in both directions. |
| Make an adjacent-floor trip. | The car slows in time even when it cannot reach full speed. |
| Make the longest trip. | The car reaches its configured speed and stops cleanly. |
| Register several calls. | The car finishes serving one direction before reversing for calls behind it. |
| Press a registered button again. | The existing call stays registered; a second press does not cancel it. |
| Call a second entrance at the same floor. | Only available door groups are used, including both if both calls are served together. |

## Doors and sensors

Test each entrance separately.

1. Watch a complete open and close cycle. Car and landing panels should clear the entrance and finish moving.
2. Press Door Open during a normal close. The doors should reverse and reopen fully.
3. Repeat with your avatar in the sensor area. Use a player character, rather than a loose part.
4. Leave the sensor area. The doors should wait for the configured time and try closing again.
5. Stay in the entrance through the nudge timeout. Check the warning sound and nudge closing behavior. Sensors do not reverse a nudge close.
6. Press Door Hold, wait longer than the normal open time, then press Door Close. The first control should hold the doors; the second should release the hold.
7. Check pre-opening, if enabled. Only an entrance available at the destination should begin to open.

Repeat a reopening test near the start, middle, and end of closing after changing door curves or speeds.

## Buttons, displays, and sound

| Item | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Car and hall buttons | Correct floor, entrance, and direction; lamps clear after service. |
| Floor display | Correct numeric floor or custom label, including basement and lobby. |
| Direction arrows | Travel direction or next serving direction, as intended for that display. |
| Hall lanterns and chimes | Correct floor, entrance, direction, and arrival timing. |
| Voice | Every enabled event and floor cue plays the intended words. |
| Audio range | Sounds are audible in the car and near the intended landing without reaching unnecessarily far. |

## Fire service

If you install fire-service controls, follow [Fire service](/hera/operation/fire-service) and check:

- Phase I recall from an idle car and a moving car.
- Alternate recall, if configured.
- Calls being rejected during recall.
- Doors held open at the recall floor.
- Phase II entry only after recall completes.
- One destination at a time in Phase II.
- Door Open and Door Close held to completion, including early release of each.
- Phase II Off returning the car to recall, followed by Phase I reset.

## Independent service

If you install independent-service controls, follow [Independent service](/hera/operation/independent-service) and check:

- Enabling and disabling while stopped.
- Hall and remote calls being rejected.
- Holding a destination button until all doors close.
- Releasing that button early reopening the doors and cancelling departure.
- Arrival opening and holding the correct entrance.
- Normal operation returning after the mode is turned off.

## Parking, passengers, and integrations

If parking is enabled, wait for its idle time and check the parking floor. The car should park with its doors closed. A new call should take priority over waiting to park.

If passenger welding is enabled, test boarding, release at arrival, jumping after release, seats, and respawning. Use Studio's multi-client test to check how the moving car and passengers look to another player.

For shared hall buttons, call every served floor with each car idle and with another car busy. For custom scripts, check state at startup, arrival, cancellation, and rejected requests.

The XCS bridge needs a **published, running Roblox server** for its server ID. Check dashboard status and remote controls there after the local elevator works. See [Connect XCS](/hera/integration/xcs).

## Keep a known working copy

Save the model and configuration after the tests pass. When an update causes a problem, compare it with that copy and repeat the affected tests before making further changes.
