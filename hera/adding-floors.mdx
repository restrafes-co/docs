## Add a floor

Duplicate a complete landing under `FLOORS` and give it an integer name. Move the landing assembly as a unit, then align its door assembly with the opening.

Set the center Y of `LEVEL_MAIN` to the platform center Y at the stop. Preserve the offset of the door group's `LEVEL` reference.

For example, to add floor `3` above floor `2` on a front-opening car with the default alias:

| Fixture | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| Upper terminal hall button | Use `.BUTTONS/F/D/BUTTON` at floor `3`. |
| Former terminal hall station | Convert it to an intermediate station if it now accepts both directions. |
| Car button | Add `F3` to the car panel with a direct `BUTTON` child. |

Set the [PI label](/hera/configuration/indicators#floor-labels) and [announcement](/hera/configuration/sound-and-voice#choose-announcements) if needed. Restart Play after adding the landing.

## Align a landing

An error isolated to one landing usually points to `LEVEL_MAIN` or the offset between that reference and the sill. Use the center of `CAR/.PLATFORM` as the car datum and preserve the supplied sill offset. For errors that vary by direction or trip length, check the [movement profile](/hera/configuration/motion).

## Add a rear entrance

Set up the rear entrance as group `2`:

| Item | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| Car doors | Place the assembly under `CAR/.DOORS/2`, with its own `LEVEL` and `DOORS`. |
| Sensors | Place them under `CAR/.DOORSENSORS/2`. |
| Landing doors | Add `.DOORS/2` at each landing served by the rear. The car and landing group IDs must match. |
| Alias | Set `MISCELLANEOUS.DOOR_GROUP_ALIASES.R = 2`. |
| Car button | Use a name such as `R3` for a rear call to floor `3`. |
| Hall button | Use a path such as `.BUTTONS/R/U/BUTTON` for a rear Up call. |

See [Buttons and lights](/hera/configuration/buttons) for fixture naming.

At a rear-only floor, a front call is rejected.

For temporary restrictions, keep the landing geometry in place and apply a [floor lockout](/hera/extensions/floor-lockouts).

## Remove a floor

Remove the landing and its car panel fixtures. Check the primary floor, parking, recall, PI labels, voice cues, ERSA stations and extensions for references to the removed floor. Restart Play.

## Move the complete elevator

Move the car, landings and counterweight together. Keep the sills aligned.
